## Gurometer: 10 traits

From Kavanagh and Browne, Decoding the Gurus. The skill body keeps the four that transfer best to course purchases. The full set:

1. **Galaxy-brain.** Confident claims across unrelated fields.
2. **Cultishness.** Insider language and ritual. A hard line between people inside and people outside.
3. **Antidisestablishmentarianism.** A reflex that the mainstream is wrong.
4. **Grievance-mongering.** A steady story of being squeezed by the system or by rivals.
5. **Narcissism.** The self is cast as more central than the work supports.
6. **Cassandra complex.** "I am the only one who sees this, and no one else will say it."
7. **Revolutionary theory.** A claim to have replaced the old knowledge outright.
8. **Pseudo-profound bullshit.** Language that sounds deep and cannot be restated as a concrete action.
9. **Conspiracy mongering.** A hint of a hidden force working against them, or against "the truth" they are telling.
10. **Grifting.** The money comes from selling the persona, not from a result the buyer can carry away.

You do not need all ten. Two or three, clearly present at once, are enough to slow down.

## Community decay

|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly member churn | under 5% | 5–10% | over 10% |
| Members who post in a month | over 30% | 20–30% | under 20% |
| Event attendance | over 15% | 10–15% | under 10% |

| Teaching posts vs selling posts, last 20 | mostly teaching | about even | mostly selling |
| Future event calendar | specific dates ahead | sparse | empty |

## Skool, without paying

- **Calendar.** Upcoming events, or only events that already happened?
- **Classroom.** Modules with a recent "last updated," or frozen since launch?
- **Leaderboard, 7-day vs 30-day.** If the 7-day top is almost the same as the 30-day top, a handful of people (staff, or planted accounts) may be keeping the room looking alive.
- **Pinned post age.** A pin that has not changed in months means nobody is tending the front of the room.
- **Skool Games rank.** If the owner is publicly racing MRR, you are likely looking at a growth sprint, not a retention phase.

## Creator-support platforms (Patreon, Ko-fi, Substack, and similar)

The main risks here are impersonation, and taking money without delivering. They are different from a guru who invents an identity from scratch.

- **Impersonation.** Name, photo, and bio match a real creator, and you cannot trace a link from that creator's own channel to this page. Verify in that direction only.
- **Pay, then nothing.** A new page charges, then posts rarely or stops. Most common when there is no track record outside the platform.
- **Giveaway or high-return "investment" pushed off-platform.** A pattern platform help pages warn about directly. It is not a content-quality issue.
- **Fake tier benefits.** A high tier promises personal replies, exclusive work, or physical merch, and the post history does not show delivery. You can only see this by reading before you upgrade.
